    How the alarm works

    Starting Game Mode sets a 2-hour timer and keeps your screen awake. When time is up, Daybreak sounds an alarm and vibrates until you press and hold the stop button. Keep Daybreak open with your volume up. Phones pause web apps that are closed or locked, so if time runs out while it's hidden, the alarm goes off the moment you come back.
